# Sales AI Agent — Client Involvement

> Created: April 1, 2026 | Updated: April 1, 2026

## Discovery & Onboarding

- **Kick-off call** — Introduction, project overview, and alignment on goals
- **Workshop session(s)** — Deep dive into the client's inbound sales process, ICP, qualification criteria, and handoff rules. Number of sessions depends on the complexity of the sales process and the scope of what the agent is expected to handle
- **Brief** — Halo Lab prepares a project brief based on discovery and workshop findings

## During Implementation

- **Weekly calls** — Status updates, demos, and feedback sessions
- **Updates 2-3 times a week** — Progress updates via messenger (e.g., Slack or other corporate messenger preferred by the client)
- **Reviews** — The client reviews conversation flows, qualification logic, and knowledge base content at key milestones

## Before Launch

- **Demo of the AI agent** — Halo Lab walks the client through the working agent
- **Instructions** — The client receives documentation on how to access conversation data, analytics, and platform features

## Time Commitment

The client's main involvement is during discovery (workshop sessions, providing sales materials and access to tools). During build and testing, involvement is lighter — reviewing progress and providing feedback. Halo Lab handles the technical work end-to-end.
