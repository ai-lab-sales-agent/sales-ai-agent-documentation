# Sales AI Agent — Security & Data Handling

> Created: April 1, 2026 | Updated: April 1, 2026

## Conversation Data

All conversation data (messages, visitor inputs, qualification data) is stored within the conversational AI platform used for the agent. The client can access conversation logs, analytics, and lead data directly from the platform.

## Compliance

- The platform is SOC 2 certified
- The platform is GDPR compliant
- Conversation data is not used to train AI models
- Other compliance requirements (HIPAA, ISO 27001, etc.) can be discussed on the discovery call

## Data Ownership

The client's conversation data and knowledge base content belong to the client. Halo Lab configures and maintains the agent but does not own the data generated by visitor interactions.
