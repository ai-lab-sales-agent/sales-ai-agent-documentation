# Case Study — Vendora

A Sales AI Agent deployed on a B2B SaaS company's website to qualify inbound leads, answer pre-sales questions, and book discovery calls automatically. Built and launched by Halo Lab.

> Created: May 5, 2026 | Updated: May 5, 2026

## Why This Is Relevant to Sales AI Agent Projects

- Direct example of a Sales AI Agent qualifying leads through natural conversation
- Demonstrates the full delivery process: discovery, design, build, integration, testing, launch
- Shows how the agent integrates with CRM and calendar tools in a real B2B environment

## Metadata

- **Industry:** SaaS, B2B
- **Services:** Sales AI Agent, Conversational AI, CRM Integration
- **Duration:** 6 weeks from kickoff to launch
- **Project Type:** Sales AI Agent

## About the Project

Vendora is a B2B SaaS company that provides workflow automation tools for mid-market companies. They had a steady flow of inbound website visitors but their sales team was spending too much time on unqualified leads and responding too slowly to hot prospects outside business hours.

Halo Lab built and deployed a Sales AI Agent that engages every visitor in a consultative conversation, qualifies them based on Vendora's ICP criteria, answers common pre-sales questions from a knowledge base, and books qualified leads directly into the sales team's calendar.

## Challenges

- Sales team spending 40% of their time on leads that were never going to convert
- Average response time to inbound leads was 6+ hours
- Zero coverage outside business hours (losing international leads in different time zones)
- No structured qualification data — sales managers walked into calls without context

## Our Approach

- Discovery workshop to map Vendora's sales process, ICP, and qualification criteria
- Designed conversation flows: discovery, qualification, objection handling, and meeting booking
- Built a knowledge base covering Vendora's products, pricing frameworks, integrations, and common questions
- Integrated with HubSpot CRM for lead data sync and Cal.com for meeting booking
- Ran 60+ test conversations to validate qualification accuracy and edge case handling
- Launched on production website with chat widget

## Results

- Agent responds to every visitor in under 5 seconds, 24/7
- Qualified leads reach the sales team with a structured pre-call brief
- Sales team reports fewer unqualified calls
- Specific ROI metrics and conversion percentages are confidential per client agreement
