## About the Project

WillWise is an AI-powered assistant for Slack built and released by Halo Lab as an internal product. It helps teams and individuals work smarter without leaving Slack — summarizing conversations, drafting announcements, answering questions using workspace knowledge, and keeping everyone focused and aligned.

## Challenges

Teams lose time scrolling through long Slack threads to find key decisions, context, or action items. Important information gets buried, and writing announcements or answering repeated questions pulls people away from focused work.
