# Competitive Differentiation

> Created: April 1, 2026 | Updated: April 1, 2026

## How Halo Lab Compares

### vs. Off-the-Shelf Chatbot Tools

Chatbot platforms with built-in lead capture typically offer forms, canned responses, and routing rules. They work for collecting contact details but are not designed for sales conversations.

A Sales AI Agent is a different category:

- **Consultative dialogue, not forms** — The agent asks discovery questions, responds to context, and adapts the conversation based on the visitor's answers
- **Qualification logic, not routing rules** — Leads are scored against the client's ICP using a structured framework, not routed by dropdown selections
- **Knowledge-grounded answers** — The agent answers questions from a curated knowledge base specific to the client's services, pricing, and process — not canned auto-replies

### vs. Building It In-House

Conversational AI platforms are accessible, but building an effective sales agent requires more than platform access:

- **Conversation design** — Structuring discovery flows, objection handling, nurture paths, and handoff logic into a coherent experience that actually qualifies leads
- **Prompt engineering** — Writing and tuning prompts so the agent stays on track, follows guardrails, and handles edge cases (off-topic questions, sensitive data, abusive messages)
- **Knowledge base architecture** — Structuring content so the AI retrieves accurate, relevant answers without hallucinating or mixing topics
- **Qualification framework design** — Defining scoring criteria, data points, and decision rules that match the client's sales process
- **Testing and iteration** — Running 50+ test conversations to find failure modes and tune the agent before it talks to real leads

Accuracy rates and qualification percentages vary per project and are not published as standardized metrics.

### vs. Other Agencies or Freelancers

Many agencies and freelancers build chatbots or AI assistants as part of a broader offering. The difference:

- **Inbound sales is the only focus** — The entire service is designed around one use case, not adapted from a support bot or general-purpose assistant template
- **Built around the client's process** — Halo Lab gathers requirements during discovery and builds the qualification framework, conversation flows, and handoff logic to reflect how the client's team actually sells
- **Post-launch optimization included** — The agent is tuned after launch based on real conversation data, not delivered and left to the client to manage

### vs. Doing Nothing (Keeping the Contact Form)

A contact form captures a name and email. It does not qualify the lead, answer their questions, or book a meeting. The result:

- Leads wait hours or days for a response — many move on to a competitor before the sales team replies
- Every lead reaches the sales team regardless of fit — no filtering, no qualification data, no context
- Outside business hours, leads get no response at all
- The sales team walks into calls without knowing anything about the visitor's needs, budget, or timeline

A Sales AI Agent responds in seconds, qualifies the lead in real time, answers their questions from a knowledge base, and hands off to the sales team with full context — 24/7.
