# Case Study Questions — AI Workforce Platform

> Created: April 1, 2026

## Metadata
1. What's the product/project name?
2. Client name — or is it under NDA?
3. Website URL (if public)?
4. Duration — when did it start, is it ongoing?

## About the Project
5. Is this a SaaS platform where businesses sign up and create agents themselves, or does Halo Lab build the agents for each client?
6. What does "executes work autonomously" mean concretely — does the agent take actions in external tools (CRM, ad platforms, email), or does it generate outputs for a human to review and send?
7. Lead generation — what kind? Cold outreach, inbound qualification, LinkedIn scraping, something else?
8. Content creation — what type of content? Blog posts, social media, ad copy, email sequences?
9. Advertising — does the agent manage ad campaigns (budgets, targeting), create ad creatives, or both?
10. Are there other agent types beyond lead gen, content, and advertising?
11. Does the end user (the business) need technical skills to set up their agents, or is it no-code/guided?
12. What integrations does it connect to — CRMs, ad platforms, social media, email tools?

## Challenges
13. What problem did the client have before this platform? What was manual, slow, or not working?
14. What were the client's expectations — what did they want the platform to achieve?

## Our Approach
15. What did Halo Lab's team actually do? (design, development, AI architecture, integrations?)
16. Were there phases — MVP first, then expansion?
17. What AI/tech stack was used? (LLMs, frameworks, integrations)

## Results
18. Any measurable outcomes — users on the platform, agents created, tasks automated?
19. Is the platform live? Beta? In production with real businesses?
